**Decimal to Fraction Program**
Note: Some decimals will not solve.  Press the "ON" button to turn the calculator off.
      Press the "ON" button to turn the calculator back on.  You can then clear the calculator
      and resume using the calculator.  The results are pushed into the stack so they appear
      in the order you would read/write/say the value.  I did this so that on the real HP15C
      calculator you will see the Numerator R-dn Denominator or Integer R-dn Numerator R-dn
      Denominator.  Some fractions will take longer to solve than others.
1. With a decimal value in the X register of the stack press f A
Result:  Y: Denominator
         X: Numerator
Note: This could be an improper fraction with the numerator greater than the denominator.  Pressing R/S
      will convert to a mixed fraction.  If the initial X register value is negative, the negative sign
      will be appended to the denominator.
2. Press R/S
Result:  Z: Denominator
         Y: Numerator
         X: Integer
Note: The calculator will evaluate if an improper fraction and if so, convert to a mixed fraction.  If
      not an improper fraction, the program returns with no change to the stack.  If the initial X
      register value is negative, the negative sign will be appended to the integer & denominator.
      These do not cancel.  They simply mean the mixed fraction is negative.



**Greatest Common Divisor**
1. With 2 numbers loaded in X and Y of the stack, press f B
Result:  X: GCD



**Least Common Multiple**
1. With 2 numbers loaded in X and Y of the stack, press f C
Result:  X: LCM
